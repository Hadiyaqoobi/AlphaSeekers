How It Works

Our Weekly Rhythm

Simple and practical — no corporate jargon, just getting things done:

When What How It Works
Monday Planning Meeting Each person shares: (1) What I'll work on this week (2) What I need from others (3) Any problems I'm stuck on
Wednesday Quick Check-In Quick round: Am I on track? Need help? Any updates? — Keep it short and focused
Friday Update (async) Short message in our group chat: What I finished this week. No meeting needed — just a quick text update
End of Month Monthly Review Look back at what we accomplished. Plan next month's priorities. Share student feedback. Celebrate progress.

The goal is simple: every week we decide together what needs to happen, and every week we deliver. Small steps, consistently.

Proposal — Let's Discuss

Building Our Advisory Board

As we grow, we need experienced people to guide us — not to run the organization, but to advise us. Here's why:

Why a Board?

Every successful nonprofit has advisors
Funders expect to see a board structure
We need guidance on fundraising, legal, and strategy
Board members open doors to partnerships
It gives AlphaSeekers credibility and trust
Organizations like SOLA and Khan Academy built boards early

Advisory vs. Governing Board

We're starting with an Advisory Board
They advise — they don't make decisions for us
Sahar and the team still run the organization
Advisors share expertise, connections, and mentorship
As we grow, this can evolve into a formal governing board

Who We'd Want on the Board

Education Expert — Someone with experience in online learning or Afghan education
Nonprofit Leader — Someone who has built or run a nonprofit
Fundraising Advisor — Experience with grants and donor relationships
Community Voice — Someone from the Afghan diaspora community
Tech Advisor — Guidance on platform growth and scaling

Board Responsibilities

Meet quarterly (4 times a year) for 1-2 hours
Review our progress and give strategic feedback
Help identify funding and partnership opportunities
Mentor Sahar and the leadership team
Advocate for AlphaSeekers in their networks
No financial commitment required — just expertise and time

Let's discuss

  • Do you know anyone who could be a good advisor for us?
  • What kind of expertise do we need most right now?
  • Any questions about how a board works?

Working Agreement

Our Promise to Each Other

These aren't rules from the top — these are agreements we make as a team:

1. Show up when you say you will

If you commit to 10 hours, we plan around that. If something changes, just communicate early.

2. Communicate openly

Stuck? Say it. Overwhelmed? Say it. Have a better idea? Say it. No judgment.

3. Deliver what you commit to

At Monday's planning, we each pick our tasks. By Friday, we share what we completed. Simple.

4. Students come first

Everything we do should serve our students. When in doubt, ask: "Does this help a student?"

5. Support each other

We're a small team. When someone needs help, we step in. No silos.

6. Grow together

As AlphaSeekers grows, your role grows. The people who build this from day one are the future leaders.
