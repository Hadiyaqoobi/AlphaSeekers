# AlphaSeekers — Competitive Design Research

> Research conducted across 10 leading education and nonprofit platforms to inform the AlphaSeekers redesign.

---

## 1. Khan Academy (khanacademy.org)

### Layout Structure
- **Contained** max-width ~1200px, but with full-bleed hero and colored section backgrounds
- Alternates between full-bleed colored sections and contained white sections

### Navigation
- Sticky, full-width, ~64px height. Solid white background with subtle border
- Logo left, centered nav, CTA right. Clean hamburger on mobile

### Hero Section
- Full-width gradient background. Text-left, visual-right split (~55/45)
- Headline ~48px, subtext ~18px. Single primary CTA

### Color Palette
- Primary Blue: `#1865F2`; Teal: `#14BF96`; Purple: `#7854AB`
- Warm background tones, not pure white
- Success green, warm accents for engagement

### Typography
- Custom "Chalky" display font for personality
- Clean sans-serif (Lato/system) for body
- H1 ~48px, Body ~16px

### Animations & Motion
- Subtle entrance animations, smooth page transitions
- No heavy parallax or scroll-triggered effects

### Unique Elements
- "Handmade" visual language — hand-drawn underlines, notebook textures
- User role-based homepage personalization
- Accessible color system with semantic tokens

## 2. Coursera (coursera.org)

### Layout Structure
- Full-width sections with contained content (~1200px max-width)
- Alternating white/light-gray sections

- Sticky, full-width, ~64px. Solid white with shadow on scroll

- Full-width with gradient/image background
- Large headline + single CTA. Clean, conversion-focused

## DESIGN SYNTHESIS

### TOP 5 Design Patterns Across Multiple Sites

| # | Pattern | Found In |
|---|---------|----------|
| 1 | **Full-width sections with contained content** — colored/image backgrounds edge-to-edge, max-width inner container | All 10 sites |
| 2 | **Sticky full-width navbar** with transparent-to-solid scroll transition | Teach For All, Pencils of Promise, Code.org, Coursera |
| 3 | **Animated impact counters** — numbers count up from 0 when scrolled into view | Khan Academy, Code.org, Teach For All, Pencils of Promise, Room to Read |
| 4 | **Dual-persona CTA** — Student/Teacher or Donor/Volunteer pathways from hero | Code.org, ClassDojo, Khan Academy, AlphaSeekers (existing) |
| 5 | **Human-centric photography** with dignified, authentic representation | Pencils of Promise, Room to Read, Teach For All, Preply |

### Typography System

| Element | Font Family | Size | Weight | Line-Height |
|---------|------------|------|--------|-------------|
| **H1 (Hero)** | Plus Jakarta Sans | `clamp(2.5rem, 5.5vw, 4rem)` (40–64px) | 800 | 1.08 |
| **H2 (Section)** | Plus Jakarta Sans | `clamp(1.75rem, 3.5vw, 2.75rem)` (28–44px) | 700 | 1.12 |
| **H3 (Card)** | Plus Jakarta Sans | `clamp(1.25rem, 2vw, 1.5rem)` (20–24px) | 700 | 1.25 |
| **H4 (Sub)** | Plus Jakarta Sans | 1.125rem (18px) | 600 | 1.35 |
| **Body** | Inter | 1rem (16px) | 400 | 1.65 |
| **Body Large** | Inter | 1.125rem (18px) | 400 | 1.6 |
| **Small/Caption** | Inter | 0.875rem (14px) | 500 | 1.5 |
| **Kicker** | Inter | 0.75rem (12px) | 700 | 1 |
| **Arabic/Dari** | Noto Sans Arabic | Same scale | Same | Same |

**Font Choices:**
- **Plus Jakarta Sans** — Modern geometric display font with personality. Rounded terminals give warmth. Excellent x-height for readability. Free on Google Fonts.
- **Inter** — Best-in-class UI body font. Optical sizing support, excellent at small sizes. Free on Google Fonts.
- **Noto Sans Arabic** — Full Dari/Farsi support with compatible metrics.

### Animation Strategy

| Element | Animation | Trigger | Duration |
|---------|-----------|---------|----------|
| Hero content | Staggered fade-up (headline→subtext→CTA→image) | Page load | 150ms interval, 600ms each |
| Section content | Fade-up + translateY(30px→0) | Scroll into viewport | 500ms |
| Stat counters | Count from 0 to target | Scroll into viewport | 1500ms |
| Button hover | scale(1.03) + shadow increase | Hover | 200ms |
| Card hover | translateY(-6px) + shadow elevation | Hover | 250ms |
| Navbar | transparent→white/blur background | Scroll past hero | 300ms |
| Mobile menu | Full-screen slide-in from right | Hamburger click | 300ms |
| "Learn" underline | SVG path draw animation | Page load (1s delay) | 800ms |
| Floating badges | translateY oscillation (±6px) | Continuous | 4s cycle |
| Section kicker bar | Width grows from 0 to full | Scroll into viewport | 400ms |
